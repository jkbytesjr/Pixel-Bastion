# Pixel Bastion 1.4

**Online co-op is here.** Team up with up to three friends and fight through the same dungeon together, right in your browser. There's nothing to install and no account to make.

## Play together

1. On the title screen, choose **Co-op**, type your name and pick **Host a game**.
2. Share the 5-letter room code, or click **Copy invite link** and send it to your friends.
3. Friends choose **Co-op** and enter the code, or just open your link.
4. Click **Start run**. Friends can also join after the run has started.

## How co-op works

- **Your own hero.** Everyone keeps their own character, gear, level and attributes, and your hero responds instantly on your screen.
- **Shared rewards.** Every kill gives XP to the whole party, and loot goes to whoever walks over it.
- **Tougher monsters.** Monsters go after the nearest hero and get 60% more health for each extra player.
- **Down, not out.** If you fall, you're back on the next floor. The run only ends when the whole party is down, and then the host can start it again for everyone.
- **Together through the portal.** One hero stepping into the portal takes the whole party to the next floor.
- **Stay in touch.** Name tags float over your friends' heroes, and a party list shows everyone's health and the room code.

Co-op doesn't pause, isn't saved, and plays with mods off so everyone's game matches.

## Good to know

- Players connect directly to each other over WebRTC. The free public PeerJS service is only used to help players find each other.
- A very strict school or work network can block the connection.
- Everyone needs the same version of the game, so if joining fails, reload the page and try again.

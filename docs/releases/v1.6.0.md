# Pixel Bastion 1.6

**Every floor now hides a rift into another dimension.** Find it, break its two seals and step through.

## The hidden rift

- **Hidden.** The rift waits in a small room behind a cracked wall. Until its seals are broken, it's just a dark stone ring between two standing stones.
- **Seal 1, the rift plates.** Step on three coloured plates in the order shown by an obelisk with a violet crystal on top. A wrong plate resets them.
- **Seal 2, the brazier trial.** Four violet braziers stand in the corners of a room. Touch one to light it, and the clock starts. Light all four before the flames die, or they all go dark and you start over. A meter at the top of the screen shows the time left.
- **Awakening.** Each broken seal lights a gem on the standing stones. When both are lit, the rift swirls to life, its wall glows violet and it appears on your minimap.
- **Another dimension.** Inside is a floating pocket world with an elite-led pack and two rich chests. The way back puts you exactly where you stepped in.

The rift replaces the open purple portal from 1.5. Vaults, the other secret rooms and the cyan shortcut portals are unchanged.

## Good to know

- In co-op, the plates and braziers work for every hero, and the rift wakes for the whole party.
- Co-op players all need version 1.6. If joining fails, reload the page.

# Pixel Bastion 1.5

**Every floor is now a puzzle as well as a fight.** Capture shrines to break the boss seals, slip through portals into pocket dimensions, hunt for secret rooms, and face bosses that hit harder and fight smarter.

## Shrines and gates

- The boss arena on each floor is sealed by gates. Find the shrine and stand in its circle until the meter fills.
- Any monster in the circle **contests** it and stops the capture, and more monsters climb out of the floor while you hold it. If you leave, the meter slowly drains.
- In co-op, more heroes in the circle capture it faster.

## Portals, puzzles and secrets

- **Pocket dimensions.** A purple mini-portal leads to a sealed pocket world with an elite pack and a rich chest. The way back returns you to exactly where you stepped in.
- **Shortcuts.** Some floors have a cyan portal pair linking distant rooms.
- **Plate vaults.** Step on three coloured plates in the order the obelisk blinks to open a vault. A wrong plate resets the puzzle.
- **Secret rooms.** A faintly glowing crack marks a hidden wall. Hit it twice, with a sword or an arrow, to break through to the loot behind it.

## Tougher bosses

- Bosses have much more health and hit harder, especially deeper down.
- They get faster below half health and again below a quarter.
- Each boss has two new telegraphed powers, such as earthquake rings, boulder tosses, arrow rain, a shadow-step teleport, flame walls, meteors, bone prisons and death spirals. Watch the warning zones on the ground.

## Tactical combat

- **Stamina.** Dodge rolls use stamina (the green bar). A full bar gives about three rolls in a row.
- **Exposed.** Monsters are off balance just after they attack (a yellow marker appears) and take 50% more damage.
- **High ground.** Attacking from a ledge or altar deals 20% more damage. Attacking from below deals 15% less.
- **Elements.** Weapon powers deal fire, frost, lightning or force. Every monster has weaknesses and resistances, and the game tells you the first time you find one.
- **Smarter monsters.** Monsters flank you and take turns attacking. Archers and casters sidestep your shots, spiders pounce when you're busy, and shieldbearers turn to block attacks from behind.

## Good to know

- Co-op keeps shrines, gates, puzzles and secret walls in sync for everyone.
- Co-op players all need version 1.5. If joining fails, reload the page.
